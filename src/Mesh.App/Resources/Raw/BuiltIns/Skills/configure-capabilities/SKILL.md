# Instructions

1. Identify the intended audience and the concrete task they need to perform.
2. Decide whether the audience is the owner, an approved contact, a circle of contacts, or a public service.
3. Select only the user-created Knowledge, Skills, widgets, connector folders, and tools required for that task.
4. Prefer circle grants for repeated contact groups and explicit service attachments for public capabilities.
5. Remember that a Skill supplies workflow guidance but never grants a Tool permission by itself.
6. Review the resulting scope for private information or capabilities that are broader than necessary.
7. Test with a harmless representative request from the intended role and confirm that unrelated assets are absent.
8. Tighten or remove grants that are not required. Built-in content is internal and cannot be shared or configured through these controls.
